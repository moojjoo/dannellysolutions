Privacy notice

What this notice covers

This notice is for dannellysolutions.com, the consulting website operated by Dannelly Solutions Inc. It does not cover RecruiterReply or our Blogger blog. Check each site's notices for how it handles information.

When you contact us

The contact form asks for your name, work email, company, service, timeline, and project summary. You can also provide a budget range and contact preferences, such as your timezone. We use this information to respond to your inquiry and discuss the services you request. If you email us directly, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send.

The form also sends a start timestamp, a spam-check field and a verification token. AWS Lambda processes the request, Cloudflare Turnstile verifies the token and may receive your IP address during verification, and Amazon SES delivers the inquiry to our business inbox. Please don't include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information in your message.

Website delivery and optional analytics

Amazon Web Services hosts and delivers the site. When your browser requests a page or uses the form, the services handling that request receive technical information such as your IP address and browser request details.

If you allow analytics, we use Google Analytics to understand site usage. Google Analytics can collect information such as pages viewed, interactions, device and browser information, and approximate location. We do not intentionally send the contents of the contact form to Google Analytics. You can use the website and send an inquiry with analytics off.

See the cookie and storage notice and analytics preferences.
