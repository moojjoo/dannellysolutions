Cookies and storage

Optional Google Analytics

Analytics is off until you allow it. If enabled, Google Analytics may set first-party _ga and _ga_… cookies to distinguish browsers and maintain session state. This site's tag sets a 180-day lifetime for cookies it creates and does not extend it on every visit. Browsers can shorten that lifetime, and cookies set by an earlier configuration may have different expiry dates.

Use Analytics preferences to allow analytics or turn it off. Turning it off disables future analytics collection by the site tag and asks your browser to remove this site's known first-party Analytics cookies. It does not undo requests already made or erase information already received.

Remembering your choice

The site records your choice and its expiration in this browser's local storage under dannelly-analytics-preference-v1. The site honors that choice for up to 180 days. Your browser can clear the record sooner. This preference is not sent to our server, does not contain an identifier, and applies only to the current site address and browser.

Security and spam prevention

The homepage uses Cloudflare Turnstile for contact-form verification regardless of your Analytics choice. It is not an analytics tracker on this site. For details about Cloudflare's verification technology and any storage it may use, see Cloudflare's Turnstile Privacy Addendum.
